# David J.S. Madgett

I'm a lawyer and software developer in Minneapolis. I founded Madgett Law, LLC, where I handle consumer and credit-reporting litigation against credit bureaus and furnishers, business and investment work, and trusts and estates. I'm a UVA Law graduate and a former Air Force JAG.

What I build is tooling for running a law practice with AI assistants: specialist agents with written job descriptions, a citation check that reads the actual opinion, a time record rebuilt from the work itself. Most of it is plain text in a folder, and the right move is to write your own.

The reason is arithmetic. Most people with a real legal problem never see a lawyer because an hour of legal help costs more than they have. If the cost of producing legal work comes down, more of those files get taken.

[Claude Command](https://github.com/Davemadgett/claude-command) is the first piece worth packaging. It's a kanban board over your Claude Code sessions, for the Mac: every session in flight on one screen, each carrying the client, case or project it belongs to.

I write about how all of this works, for other lawyers, in the [Practice Lab](https://www.madgettlaw.com/practice-lab/). The software is listed at [madgettlaw.com/software](https://www.madgettlaw.com/software/).

Bugs and questions about the code go to the issues on the repository. The firm is on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/madgett-law-llc).
